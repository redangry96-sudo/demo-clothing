Questions we hear often

Can I pay when the parcel arrives?

Yes. Cash on delivery is available across Pakistan. You can check the parcel before paying the rider.

How long does delivery take?

Usually two to four working days. Delivery is free on orders above ₨3,000, otherwise it is ₨200.

What if the size does not fit?

Message us on WhatsApp within seven days. We exchange unworn pieces with tags attached.

Are the clothes the same as the photos?

Yes. If a piece looks different from what you ordered, we replace it or return your money.
